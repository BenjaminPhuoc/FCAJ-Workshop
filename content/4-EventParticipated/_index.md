Over the course of the internship, I got to attend two events. Both turned out to be memorable — packed with fresh, practical technical content, plus some great gifts and moments along the way.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Multiplayer Game Networking Workshop

&emsp;**Date:** 06/06/2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Summary of Activities:**  
The session went deep on networking protocols for multiplayer games (HTTP Polling, UDP, WebSocket), then walked through the full build of a Serverless WebSocket system on AWS (API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, CloudWatch), backed by a live client-side networking demo in Godot Engine. It also covered containerizing applications with Docker.

&emsp;**Key Takeaways & Value Gained:**  
* **Technical Knowledge:** Picked up how to design Serverless networking architectures for games, model non-relational data in DynamoDB, and write Dockerfiles to package apps into isolated sandbox environments.
* **Systems Mindset:** Came away with a much clearer sense of the trade-offs between performance, reliability, and operating cost in system design.
* **Practical Experience:** Built a stronger habit of watching cloud costs, learned how to handle exceptions like sudden disconnects, and sketched out ways to bring these techniques into my own personal and academic projects.


### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)
&emsp;**Event Name:** Cloud Architect Competition Finals & Tech Talk

&emsp;**Date:** 06/11/2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Summary of Activities:**  
This one had two parts: the Cloud Architect competition finals (scenario-based multiple-choice rounds on AWS architecture) and a Tech Talk series from industry speakers, covering AI/multi-agent frameworks in cloud security (DevSecOps), enterprise SLA governance and monitoring with CloudWatch/SNS, and a prep roadmap for the AWS CLF-C02 exam.

&emsp;**Key Takeaways & Value Gained:**  
* **Domain Knowledge:** Learned how advanced AWS services fit together at enterprise scale, got a look at security-automation trends driven by generative AI, and realized monitoring's real purpose is keeping the customer journey unbroken while honoring SLA commitments.
* **Practical Skills:** Learned to build custom CloudWatch dashboards, configure metric alarms, and set up SNS alerts for database disconnects — plus picked up process-of-elimination and keyword-mapping techniques for tackling the AWS Cloud Practitioner exam with more confidence.
* **Personal Growth:** Sharpened my observation and critical-thinking skills working through real architecture scenarios, which helped clarify my career direction toward Cloud/DevOps engineering.
